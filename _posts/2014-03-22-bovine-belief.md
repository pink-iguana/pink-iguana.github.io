**Iguana:**
_You can't know me. All you will ever see is a mask. Even if you realise this, you have gone further than most. You will see what you want to see in me. If you can't, you never wanted to see me thus._

Do I see a jaundiced cow buffalo cocktail?

**Jaundiced cow buffalo cocktail:**
Oh my dear wagtail,
In your ignorance of mocktails,
You compare me with swine and buffaloes!
But I am only a champion of these poor fellows!

I am Sphinx.
The preacher of hoodwinks.
I am the arachnid of words,
The envy of Neith and the deity of nerds!

**Iguana:**
_So adept a preacher that he is hoodwinked by his own words._

You are telling me that you are the fabled riddle-spewing halfwit reduced to the level of a lion sporting a cow's head? I think you are a wannabe who is vying to be another wannabe.

**Wannabe for being a wannabe:**
For my love of animals,
Shall I allow myself –
To allow you to call me a Fauna-be.
And because you possess wit enough to not call me witless, I will permit myself to let you live. As regards to my current state, I may stoop to your level…actually literally since I am so huge; hmm…how amusing indeed…

I will give myself the authority to permit myself to bend to your level and inform you that a sphinx can put on the head of any animate in this world. Therefore only myself and I are the true connoisseurs of disguises in the entire pharaohland; or the way you choose to revere me, I am a Fauna-be. In fact, I am _the_ Fauna-be because I am only two of my kind in this cosmos.

To prove my claim of being the Sphinx, let me ask you a riddle. Prove that every whole number which is such that when 2 is subtracted from it, the answer is still greater than zero and you can make that number by combining groups of twos whole number of times, can be expressed as a sum of two numbers which if represented as a product of many numbers contain only two numbers, one –

**Iguana:**
You are asking me to prove the Goldbach conjecture which has not been proved till now. I am now pretty sure that you are the Sphinx who used to gobble up people for not being able to solve its riddles.

**Sphinx:**
The seed of faith is sown in you. It is customary to ask the name of my follower.

**Iguana:**
I am the one who eats fruits with its seeds and then allows himself to relieve himself of those seeds after a few hours. I am Iguana.

**Sphinx:**
Oh! Iguana! Why, they are funny little lizards who can make me laugh with my approval. I approve of you.

**Iguana:**
Actually, there seems to be some confusion. I am Iguana but I am not an iguana.

**Sphinx:**
Of course! How can I possibly forget? You are Iguana-not-an-iguana.

Wait! I see someone climbing up on your tree. Who is it?

**Tree climber:**
It's me, it's me. I am Chipmunkish Owl and I heard someone talking about iguanas. I am depressed right now and I eat iguanas when I'm depressed.

**Sphinx:**
But you are neither a chipmunk nor an owl. You are a cat!

**Chipmunkish Owl:**
Obviously I'm a cat. What does my name have to do with what I am? Now wouldn't you please tell me where is the iguana hiding?

**Sphinx:**
Um well, my approved follower is Iguana-not-an-iguana and he is sitting on that bough. I think his attention is diverted for some reason.

\[To Iguana\] I think it will be kind of you to allow yourself, with my permission, of course, to be eaten by your new friend and my new follower, Chipmunkish Owl. She seems really depressed and we should be eager to help.

**Iguana:**
_A bad day in office. Here is a full retard trying to appease a cat who likes to munch on iguanas. If I were to reveal my true identity, both of these will be reduced to cinders. But when you play the game of masks, you remain masked or you perish._

I am not an iguana. Can't you trash-filled-burlap-sacks see?

_They can't._

**Sphinx:**
That is an interesting riddle, I must say. Can trash filled burlap sacks see?

\[To Chipmunkish Owl\] Why don't you munch on some monitor lizards while we discuss this riddle…er…cow to cow?

**C. Owl:**
It's okay. I think I will just sleep and continue my depressed state of affairs when I wake up.

**Sphinx:**
I like this new follower of mine. Chihuahuas are funny indeed!

But, pray, let us get down to serious business. You must be curious to know about me wearing a cow's head today, aren't you?

**Iguana:**
Yeah right.

**Sphinx:**
Mind blowing! Your devotion towards me is so intense, I feel like falling prostrate to myself. Of course one can't really do that. Not that anything is impossible for me but still…

In my stead, you can fall prostrate to me. You being a follower of mine, a sliver of my divinity resides in you and hence, you paying obeisance to me is equivalent to me paying obeisance to myself –

**Iguana:**
– Which is equivalent to you falling prostrate in front of me.

**Sphinx:**
Ah well, not quite but I will grant you that you are more intelligent than the cows that are coming to visit us. Although I must not distinguish between my followers but equality is a skewed concept in itself. Fascinating, isn't it? – The things that I say.

So now you know why I wear a cow's face. It's only to make them feel at home and not burn them in the brightness of my aura.

**Iguana:**
_Or to make them realise that the truckloads of refuse coming out of the half-breed's mouth is still coming out of a bovine mouth._

I think I see them. They look like a swarm of brown ants with hairy tails. And their leader looks like a pox infected emaciated hippopotamus with angry patches of white blossoming out of the brown mildewed coat of hers.

**Sphinx:**
How do you know the leader is a cow and not a bull? Oh…of course \[blushes\]. These creatures seem to follow matriarchy. Nice, nice. I'm a feminist myself.

Ah…here you come!
Welcome!
To this humble yet grand abode of Sphinx,
The protector of cows, lions and the lynx!
The inspiration for the Trojan Horse
And the creator of the code of Morse,
Known to its followers as the Lord of Riddles,
The Fauna-be and…\[pauses, unsure\]…the winner of gold medals!

**Pox infected emaciated hippopotamus:**
O mighty Fauna-be, we the Cows of Navarone, come to your service. I, Crispy Chimichanga Tortilla, on behalf of the entire clan, would like to offer you my oblation of milk and my…er…prostrate?
\[Falls flat, not exactly but as far as cows can go\]

We have heard about your mightitude and on sighting you, I can harbour no doubt about what we have heard. The speed with which you stand, if I had any doubt about your divinity, it would be blast-famous. We beseech you to allow us to ingress a part of your divinity in our beef and help us launch Project Bovine Belief.

**Sphinx:**
Miss C. C. Tortilla, let me first introduce you –

**C. C. Tortilla:**
Mrs Tortilla, if you please. I offered you my oblation of milk remember? Please do not call my son, Corny Enchilada Tortilla, a bastard.

**Sphinx:**
What has milk or missus got anything to do with calling your son a bastard?
\[Iguana chuckles, Sphinx eyes him with distaste\]

This saucy fellow that you see there, Mrs Tortilla, is my new follower Iguana-not-an-iguana. The one slumbering on a bough near him is my cherished devotee, Chipmunkish Owl. The buxom leader of cows \[C. C. Tortilla blushes\] that you behold in front of you, my dear Iguana, is Mrs Horny Chimichanga Tortilla, may her soul rest in peace. Now that we are done with futile rituals, let us start this session whose sole purpose is the emancipation of cows which will inject lactose energy in the Non-Lactosian sectors.

**C. C. Tortilla:**
Oh Lord Sphinx! The speed with which you orate gives me the creeps even in my horns.

**Sphinx:**
If there is an element in your body that is not moved by my words, that element is not living at all. It is as true as the constancy of the Polaris. But let us get down to serious business. First of all, all of you must ensure that humans consider cow meat as sacred and let it always be called divine beef henceforth.

**Iguana:**
Or you could ban beef altogether.

**Sphinx:**
Oh no my dear Iguana, that would not be wise. The name of their party would lose all relevance; "The Divine Beef Party", that is what they call themselves.

**C. C. Tortilla:**
But…but you only told us to name it thus.

**Sphinx:**
Of course I did. I am the Lord Protector of Nature, the Fauna-be. I have to maintain the food chain which is a fundamental law of Nature. I cannot allow myself to let you meddle with the cosmic laws!

**Iguana:**
Food chain is a human concept.

**C. C. Tortilla:**
If we cannot but impede them from beefy overconsumption, can we at least have extreme supreme monopoly over dairies and their products? After all, it is we who toil to produce the raw material.

**Sphinx:**
You raise a fair point, Mrs Tortilla. We shall ruminate over it under my guidance. It is absolutely essential that those who produce the raw material that fuels an industry have unparalleled control over the industry.

**Iguana:**
Dairy products also use buffalo and goat milk.

**Sphinx:**
\[Looks perplexed\]
Well, maybe. However, you are unaware of the law of majority opinion. In law, a majority opinion is a judicial opinion agreed to by more than half of the members of a court. A majority opinion sets forth the decision of the court and an explanation of the rationale behind the court's decision. Because cows are the majority in this case, the verdict goes in their favour. But let us not get entangled in legal hassles here. I have a solution.

**C. C. Tortilla:**
You do? \[Doubtful\]

**Sphinx:**
You are a fair maiden, you are, Miss Horny Enchilada Tortilla. You are brighter than most but you can do better. Know this for posterity's sake that Sphinx always has a solution and my followers know that alright. Iguana-not-an-iguana will ascertain this if you want.
\[C. C. Tortilla raises an eyebrow, not really but does the cow equivalent of it; Iguana does not respond\]

For monopolising the dairy industry, you must ensure that only cow milk is used in dairy products and nothing else. Then and only then will you relieve yourselves from overexploitation.

**C. C. Tortilla:**
We certainly don't want to be exploitative. So we will accept the proposal you propose. But what about globally warming? Humans blame us for globally warming! That is not fair. We don't burn any fission fuels.

**Sphinx:**
That is ridiculous! You must protest against it. I know all about global warming. You don't burn coal at all. How can you possibly contribute to it?

**Iguana:**
Cows release methane which is a greenhouse gas.

**Sphinx:**
But of course! I know all about methane and Davy lamp. The Davy lamp is a safety lamp for use in flammable atmospheres, consisting of a wick lamp with the flame enclosed inside a mesh screen. It was created for use in coal mines, to reduce the danger of explosions due to the presence of methane and other flammable gases, called firedamp or minedamp.

All of you cows emit methane! You should not be allowed to live! You contribute to global warming!

**Iguana:**
The excessive methane emission is due to the slaughter houses. Humans breed cows for slaughter.

**Sphinx:**
You don't seem to have much love for humans.

**Iguana:**
I am not two breeds squashed together by humans. I was not created by them.

**Sphinx:**
Obviously. Humans don't create animals.
\[Iguana smiles smugly\]

**C. C. Tortilla:**
But we don't cause globally warming. Do we?

**Sphinx:**
A controversial issue indeed! The crux of it is only understood by Osiris and me. But I'm also the Lord of Hoodwinks and with my permission, you may commit the deceit of not mentioning it.

**C. C. Tortilla:**
But then what will be our agenda for agitating? What we do to stop them from levitating?

**Sphinx:**
Your agenda will be privacy and no taxation without representation! You must pass a decree that no human must see you in a compromising position –

**C. C. Tortilla:**
YES! No human will see us in a comprising position! The speed with which you say this, I am no doubtful that it is the truth.

**Sphinx:**
And you will have elected representatives of your own! Moreover your taxes will be in the form of the milk you provide. Which reminds me, what will your male folk do? How will bulls pay taxes?

**Iguana:**
They already work in fields.

**Sphinx:**
Nowadays tractors are used in the fields. Obviously you don't know what a tractor is. A tractor is an engineering vehicle specifically designed to deliver a high tractive effort (or torque) at slow speeds, for the purposes of hauling a trailer or machinery used in agriculture or construction.

Anyway, that won't do. I think bulls should do the milking. They are entitled to pay their taxes with their labours.

**C. C. Tortilla:**
But wouldn't that be a beach of piracy for us cows? I mean is it that necessary for our bulls to pay…taxation?

**Sphinx:**
Absolutely. I won't have them whiling away their time. Evil breeds in idle minds. They might indulge in crimes like murders, robbery and sexual assaults if they don't do the milking! Let it be milking of any cow, I don't mind, but they must work.

Hark! What's that whining noise?

**C. Owl:**
That is me, you oaf! You aggravate my depression because you exist! I am so depressed now, I have to munch on this bony iguana!

**Iguana:**
I am not an iguana! You think I am afraid of you? This is only my mask. You all will be annihilated!

**C. C. Tortilla:**
But what about Project Bovine Belief?

**Sphinx:**
Iguana, you must respect the food chain!

**Iguana:**
Don't make a move, I warn you, crazy cat!
\[C. Owl pounces\]

Holy Smokes! In the game of masks, you run, you only run…
\[Iguana scuttles away\]
