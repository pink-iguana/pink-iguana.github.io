---
layout: post
title: "Is It a Good Tree?"
date: 2022-05-02
categories: [poetry]
---

Standard issue, bright sunny day
Me standing
Under a eucalyptus
Studying
its bark, buds, leaves,
in that order,
Matching them with descriptions given in a tree guide
That I am holding
A stranger walks up to me,
an earnest face in its twenties,
asks a question,
in all seriousness,
"Is it a good tree?"
Did you hear that right?
He asked,
"Is it a good tree?"
Not:
Is it a wood tree?
Is it a rude tree?
Is it a lewd tree?
Is it a shrewd tree?
It was:
Is it,
a good tree?

What is a good tree?
For days later, the question haunted me.
Is it the Platonic ideal of a tree
whose leaves are sliced out of emeralds
and its bark is like polished plaster
with Christmas elves teeming out of its circular hollows
Perfect in every way but utterly banal in its perfection.
Is a good tree a martyr,
should it nurse the gluttonous restless vermin
that have gouged out its flesh
and are slowly draining its sap?
Do the good trees dream of an arboretum
where each tree has its time in the sun
and water enough to dip its roots as night falls?

Let me ask you,
yes, you,
you who agreed to play the role of a… tree farmer,
a tree farmer who finds my question offensive!
Please speak up,
these are your lines—
*Ahem… hem…*
*Excuse me!*
*I am not just a tree farmer!*
*I am a tree farmer par excellence,*
*I only breed good trees!*
*Look at these trees*—
*You can pet them*—
*Wee little saplings, when I found them,*
*I taught them how to wear mittens*
*to hold the blistering sunlight*
*in their downy canopy*
*then carefully decant it down to the earth below.*
*Sometimes I spotted them without their mittens*
*in broad daylight,*
*touching each other tenderly,*
*I immediately chided them:*
*Abstain, restrain, contain!*
*Good trees only fondle in the secrecy of night.*
*If you spread your seed everywhere,*
*do you think Santa will visit you this time?*
*I have taught them well*
*They are good trees*
*because they are my trees,*
*it's as they say,*
*The apple doesn't fall far from the tree,*
*except that I am the tree in this case,*
*and these are argyle apples.*
That's… enough of that, thank you.

Argyle apples.
Maybe they are good trees
but am I a good tree?
A good tree has poise
but I am teetering, off-balance
Like a gently rocking boat in a placid lake
or a cacophonous wind chime in a howling gale
unable to rein in the wind
unable to bind the soil
when the floodwaters sweep the earth below my feet
Still flowing, floating, drifting
long after the deluge has passed
My foliage is long
but I have lost my lustre, hunger, and zest
A knife sharpened too often
I became brittle and unstable
So shake me, shake me harder,
more words will fall off
A bad tree is still a good cautionary story.

A bizarre story.
How does it end?
I looked up from the tree guide
and said, "It's a eucalyptus."
He said, "Oh…"
"Splendid."
