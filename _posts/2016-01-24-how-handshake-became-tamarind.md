*A wizard accidentally becomes immortal. He has the idea to become the antagonist so that a hero will come along and defeat him so he can rest in peace. Sadly, the heroes are weak in comparison so the wizard creates a persona as a 'wise teacher' to train these heroes in order to defeat him. Will he succeed or is he forever doomed to endlessness?*

"What is reality?"

"A spacetime continuum? Or as described in the modern theories, a quantized version of it?"

Bonobo sighed. The girl knew too much physics for her own good. But he was not one of those fabled masters who decided right away that the apprentice did not have the aptitude, after asking a trick question.

"So my dear pupil Tamarind, you already see that there are at least two versions of reality. A classical and a quantized one."

"Who is Tamarind? My name is Handshake."

"Yes. However, Bonobo-Handshake would be a little scandalous, don't you think? I prefer Tamarind."

"Whether you prefer Tamarind or not, I am Tamarind. How can anyone be called Handshake? That's stupid."

Bonobo raised an eyebrow. Tamarind continued, "Two versions of reality? So you are saying that there can be multiple realities?"

"I am saying that there can be any reality that you want there to be."

"You as in me or anyone out there?"

"There is no centre of the universe, is there?"

Tamarind could not decide whether it was a rhetorical question. She looked on.

"Reality is like a collective consciousness. It is not a crystallised set of events in a chronological order. It is amorphous and changes with what we think. Imagine a beehive. What makes the queen bee different from the drones and the workers? The queen is not just a queen in her head. She actually looks different too. This is because she has convinced herself and the other bees that she is the queen. Of course, she might not have done it consciously. But as humans, we are capable of consciously changing reality. However, most of us do not have the conviction to do so because to convince others, you must first be good enough to convince yourself. I am a wizard because I have that kind of conviction, at least in certain matters. For example, I changed your name just some time back. Of course, even now I have convinced you that I actually did so. You might always have been Tamarind and not Handshake, in reality. But then what is reality?"

Tamarind suppressed an incredulous gasp. She did not want to be the victim of those pranks where people said some crazy things and you believed them and later they told you it was all a joke. Bonobo could certainly be doing that to her. He was a wizard and they were reputed to be jerks. If this was a prank, she was game too. "You are not a bonobo."

That should have put him off his stride. She said it with as much conviction as she could muster. Instead, Bonobo just smiled wistfully. "I have tried that before. It sort of works but does not fulfil the intended purpose."

"What?"

"Well, I thought you were trying to give me a better name than this one. However, I already know that I am not a bonobo. But my name can still be Bonobo, right? My master gave me that name as punishment for converting his goat into a gecko. And however hard I try, I can't seem to change my name."

"I see…so you had a master too?"

"At least I believe I had one."

Tamarind considered this for some time and then said, "Have you tried convincing yourself that you did not commit the crime that you are supposed to have been punished for?"

Bonobo nodded. "Tried that too. But the others weren't convinced about it and they kept reminding me. So over time, I internalised that crime. And in fact, this is what I wanted to talk to you about. When I changed your name to Tamarind, I did not just convince you about it. I also coerced other people who know you to think that your name is Tamarind even though they were not present. Obviously, it is much more difficult to do so because you cannot employ words for it. My master was able to create a memory for everyone who knew me that I was to be called Bonobo as chastisement. The point is that if enough bees are convinced that you are the queen, you become the queen. The more the number of bees, the more it is difficult for you to convince yourself otherwise."

Tamarind realised that either Bonobo was a quack or he must be extremely powerful if he had indeed convinced everyone of her name being Tamarind. What if he made her into a wood-chucking woodchuck? What if she was a wood-chucking woodchuck made to believe that she is human? What if Bonobo was an actual bonobo? And most importantly, she asked, "Why did you not ask me what I wanted my name to be? It's not fair!"

"Oh yes, I feel awful about that," he said with a straight face but then relented, "Okay I will name you whatever you want. I just thought of Tamarind because it suited your complexion."

"What if you converted me into a squirrel?"

"Possible but too difficult. I would have to convince so many people to think that you are a squirrel. In general, people are harder to convince than goats."

Tamarind wondered if Bonobo's fascination for converting goats into geckos was the crux of this discussion. She asked, "What do you want from me?"

"Ah yes. I wanted to see if I could train you to become a powerful sorcerer. Maybe, just maybe, after years of training, you will deliver me from my curse. It so happened that after training with my master, when I went out into the world, I could perform amazing feats. I could even reduce a battle ground to a human wasteland as I grew in stature. I came out unscathed from the most perilous circumstances. And then all of a sudden, a rumour started. People started saying that I am immortal. As my fame spread far and wide, everybody started believing it. Eventually, this rumour caught up with me as well and just like that, I became immortal. Still, my body is wasting away eternally. Immortality has become a curse for me."

Bonobo looked grave and ancient but Tamarind could only regard him with suspicion. She simply said, "That's stupid."

Bonobo blinked for a few seconds and then said, "Wow…that is really stupid. I think that should do it. Thanks."

"Well then, I will be on my way. By the way, Tamarind is nice. I will keep it."
