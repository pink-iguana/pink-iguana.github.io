There was a new challenger in town. They used to get them once in a while. Every world-conquering sportsperson worth her salt had to prove her mettle in the stadia of Nikuma. There was no greater honour for a sportsperson than winning against a protégé of the Sportsperson Supreme who had the support of an extremely partisan crowd. Nikuma herself was an important factor to contend with. It was said that many a challenger had wilted after being exposed to her fierce gaze. There was something different about this new challenger though.

Nikuma was the land of sports. Every sport was celebrated and had a passionate following in some corner of the dominion or another. But they had their favourites. And a challenger who dared to compete with the good people of Nikuma at one of their favourite sports received much notoriety. Even among these few brave hearts, this new challenger was unique.

Didas the jester. Didas the wanker. Didas the upstart. Didas the poser. Didas… the challenger.

His reputation had grown among the masses. When the word got to Nikuma, she suddenly rose from her throne and demanded, "I am the creator of Nikuma. I was the one who inscribed the sacred rules of Nikuma in the purest sanctum of this land, the Locker Room. I forged the first paddles with my bare hands. I am the Sportsperson Supreme and I fashioned champions out of those first players of Nikuma. It took blood, sweat and tears to create the best Nikuma players this world has ever known. So much sacrifice. To challenge us at Nikuma is to ridicule those sacrifices. No one has ever done it. Who has the audacity to do it now?"

"I am Didas, your majesty," said a scrawny youth.

"So *you* are the Didas?"

"Just a Didas, ma'am. Just another Didas."

"And how does just-a-Didas decide to challenge the mighty Nikuma players of this land?"

"On one condition. If I lose, I do not starve like the elite players under your patronage do."

"The nerve! You come here as a foreigner challenging the players of our sacred sport and then demand – "

"Not a demand, ma'am. Just a plea. I am a plebeian. I have no formal training to take on the champions in your ranks."

"Then why do it? If this is an unholy alliance between you and our rivals who want to shame us then you will pay the price with your life!"

"Not to shame you. No, ma'am. Would not think of it. When I was too little to know paddle from ball, I might have found a toy paddle lying around. My mother tells me I clenched it as tight as I could in my tiny fingers. I did not let go of it for three days! So you see, your majesty, I was born to be of service to the great sport of Nikuma. I have always wanted to travel to Nikuma and witness the stuff that makes champions out of ordinary humans, and most of all, their queen, the Sportsperson Supreme, who is known to be the greatest sportsperson that has ever lived. When I first came here, I started playing against simpletons like me. Now I have grown up playing this sport so I was able to defeat them. But it wasn't easy. They were so good compared to the people of every other country I have been to! I have defeated the best Nikuma players of every other dominion except this one. But this was always the final frontier. If the amateurs could be so good, how good would the pro players be? It made me giddy with excitement to think of all the wonderful matches I was going to play. Initially, I could not get any pro players to play against me. But one of the retired players saw me and decided to play against me. And I won! Suddenly people started noticing me. I was so happy because I thought I was finally going to get a match against an elite pro. And it did happen too. One of the pros, Mustard, needed a hitting partner. In the first match, I had no clue how to return his balls. He put so much work on them. The balls were spinning like crazy. But a couple of matches in, I started to get the hang of it. After losing three matches in a row, I got my first win. Since then, I have never lost to him. In fact, I have never lost to any pro since then. Even though I haven't played against the highest ranked players, I am now confident that I can win against anyone under your patronage."

"Every player under my patronage has a different style. Why do you think you can beat all of them?"

"There is something missing in their game and I can exploit that."

"You do not even have formal training! Do not forget that their training regimes were created by me personally!"

"You are the Sportsperson Supreme! Your prowess as a player or a coach can never be under question. But your majesty, there is something that you might have overlooked."

"There *is* something I have overlooked. Your impudence! And I am tempted not to overlook it any further! But you have piqued my curiosity. I want to see this trick of yours. I am feeling generous today so I will accept your condition as well. You may play without any fear of starvation upon losing. But if this is a ploy to shame us, then you will be executed."
