Research and writing are deeply intertwined. The act of writing forces clarity of thought — you cannot write clearly about something you do not understand clearly.

## Why Writing Matters for Researchers

Many researchers treat writing as the final step: do the work, then write it up. But I've found that writing *during* the research process is just as valuable. It helps you:

1. **Identify gaps** in your reasoning before you go too far down a path
2. **Communicate ideas** to collaborators and get early feedback
3. **Build a record** of your thinking that you can return to later

## The Blog as a Research Tool

This blog is partly a place to think out loud. Not everything here will be polished or final — some posts will be exploratory, half-formed ideas that I'm working through. I find this kind of public thinking valuable, even if it occasionally means being wrong in public.

I hope some of these posts will be useful or interesting to others working on similar problems.
