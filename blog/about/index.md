It's funny how radio stations still exist. They look like dodos alongside YouTube, Spotify, or even TV. And maybe they are going extinct too. When you can have a customised playlist of audio files, why would you listen to something that's not tailor-made for you? Yet, there is something about the radio that makes me want to go to the trouble of tuning in, perhaps a misplaced sense of adventure derived from listening to an old forgotten song or a new autotune disaster. In the exact same manner, this station (because calling it a blog is too mainstream) could be that new autotune disaster that you can't get out of your head; or, and even I know this is unlikely, an interesting way for you to put off work until you realise that further procrastination could lead to significant casualties.

### The Cover Art

The cover art was generated using OpenAI's DallE2 with the following prompt:

> *late one moonlit night*
> *the Sakyamuni*
> *walking through the streets*
> *of Benares*

These lines are from the piece [Seven Selfish Sunrises](https://elsiitk.wordpress.com/2017/01/02/seven-selfish-sunrises/) written by a friend who has since taken these and presumably many more such lines to a place where the radio reception is not quite so good.
