In case you are a hostile entity trying to mess up my lazy mathy life: no one. Go home and forget about this blog. It is not at all important in the larger scheme of things. Just forget about it. Seriously.

Otherwise: Pink Iguana. It has been my pen name for some time now. Why? Long story, don't ask. Oh, you weren't asking? What's that? You don't give two hoots about any story I have to tell. You also think I am pretentious and my sense of humour is stale.

Um, okay. At least I can do some maths. For the mathy aspect of my life, check out the [research page]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}). Some of my commissioned/published work is listed below:

- [*Chemical blue gum*](https://kindredtrees.com.au/poems/chemical-blue-gum/) for the [Kindred Trees](https://kindredtrees.com.au/) project, where Canberra-based poets are asked to write in response to a local tree.
- [*Chai*](https://belongingmag.com/2021/06/27/chai/) in [be:longing](https://belongingmag.com/), an online magazine exploring personal experiences of migration, cross-culture, nostalgia and belonging.
- *Siegel zeros* (with M. Cully-Hugill, D. Nikolic, and A. Yang) in the print journal for nonfiction poetry [RABBIT](https://rabbitpoetry.com/) (Issue 37: Collaborations) based in Melbourne.
- *Asteroid mining* in the [Good Future Zine](https://www.poeticcitycbr.com/goodfuturezine) which was compiled in March 2021 as part of the [Poetic City Festival](https://www.poeticcitycbr.com/) in Canberra.
- Excerpts from a few of my poems with some commentary have been broadcast on ArtSound FM in their weekly 'Poetry on the Radio' segment. You can find the recordings on their [webpage](https://artsound.fm/poetry-on-the-radio/) (look for Shehzad Hathi, which happens to be my name).
